Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: October 23, 2026 · Shorts Uploader

How to delete data related to an Instagram, Threads or YouTube account connected to Shorts Uploader.

Option 1. Disconnect the app (immediate)

  1. Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites → «쇼츠업로더 SNS» → Remove
  2. Threads: Settings → Account → Website permissions → «쇼츠업로더 SNS» → Remove
  3. YouTube (Google): Google Account → Security → Third-party apps → «Shorts Uploader» → Remove access

When you disconnect a Meta account, Meta notifies us and we immediately delete that account's access tokens. If you also send a data deletion request from Meta, we delete all data as in Option 2.

Option 2. Request by email

Email daejang324@gmail.com with the subject «Data deletion request» and the username (e.g. @accountname) and service of the connected account.

What we delete and when

Access tokens, account information (ID, username, profile), post performance records, and comments and private-reply logs related to that account's posts are deleted within 30 days of the request, and we confirm by email (or with a Meta confirmation code). Posts already published on Instagram, Threads or YouTube remain on those services; delete them there or ask us to do so.
